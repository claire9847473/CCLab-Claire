[ CLABY ]

Scientific Name:

Claby

Discovered by / at:

Claire, 1989

Family:

Claby belongs to a family of bioluminescent amphibious creatures that can adapt to both water and land. Claby is currently the only known species of its family.

Habitat:

So far we believe that Claby came from the depths of the ocean. When Claby started to grow cillia, it began to exhibit the surface of the ocean, which is where we first discovered it. After it discovered us, it crawled to the ground and started to explore its surroundings and land forms. When it grows into maturity, it will be able to live in both water and on land.

Appearance:

Claby is a four-eyed(two on the back) creature that glows and swims in the ocean, but turns pitch black and crawls when it reached land. In its aquatic state, driven by wave energy, it constantly transforms its shape and color and can mutate into a glowing particle swarm. In its terrestrial state, its cillia retract into rigid, grip-focused appendages that allow it to crawl.

Key Characteristics:

  • Likes interacting with humans.
  • Eats nutrients in the ocean by its cillia.

What we know so far about Claby? Little is known about Claby’s origins. Some believe it appeared after a strange change in the ocean’s ecosystem, while others think it has existed there for much longer than humans have noticed.
Guidelines for human interaction with this creature Approach Claby slowly. When you get close, it will gradually change from cool colors to warm tones and begin to glow. When there is a trigger in its surroundings, it reaches to the land and transforms to its terrestrial form. When no human is nearby, Claby becomes completely black and remains still.
